Delete your SubVora account

In the app (instant)

  1. Open SubVora and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Enter your password and confirm.

Without the app

Email gaurav2000ashish@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, subject "Delete my SubVora account". We delete the account within 7 days and reply to confirm. We may ask you to confirm the request from that same address.

What gets deleted

Everything: your email address, password hash, subscriptions, categories, payment source labels, sign-in tokens and password reset codes. Nothing is kept afterwards.

Data cached on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app.
